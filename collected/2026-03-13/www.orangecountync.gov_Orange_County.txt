In this video , Jackie Podger discusses her volunteer work on the Adult Care Home and Nursing Home Community Advisory Committee. Welcome to Breeze Farm! Breeze Farm is a 270-acre research and education farm located just north of Hillsborough, North Carolina. It is a farm incubator where aspiring and new farmers can rent land and use the facilities to improve their skills and build a customer base. To help protect farmland and farming as a way of life in rural communities consider becoming a friend of Breeze Farm .&nbsp; Need help understanding your tax bill? Property owners in Orange County, NC, began receiving their property tax bills on July 31. The bill contains the property taxes due to each taxing jurisdiction based on the location of the property.
