 Rowan Public Library’s Bookpalooza Used Book Sale Returns March 13 Through 16 

Rowan Public Library, in conjunction with Friends of Rowan Public Library, is hosting Bookpalooza, a four-day-long book sale event that begins with a preview sale for Friends of RPL members on March 13. The sale is open to the public from March 14-16. BOOKPALOOZA CONTINUED
